## Wind Waker Multiplayer v0.6.1

**New defaults in v0.6.1.** Three things that were opt-in are now on for everyone:

- **Their bombs, arrows and cannonballs.** When another player throws a bomb, shoots an arrow or fires the boat's cannon near you, you see it fly and land in your game. It can blow up walls and boulders, light torches and hit switches in your world, but it never hurts you, your boat or the enemies (each game's enemy damage still comes from the player who hit them). All players need v0.6.1. To turn it off, start the app or the command line with the environment variable `WW_PROJECTILES=off`.
- **Live switches in co-op.** A switch your partner sets in the room you are standing in now applies as soon as you stand still, instead of at your next room change. The host can turn it off in the lobby's co-op panel (Advanced: **Live switches in your room**), or with `--coop-live-switches=off` on the command line.
- **Gohdan's kill counts for everyone.** In co-op, Gohdan (Tower of the Gods) now joins Gohma, Kalle Demos, Molgera, Jalhalla and the Helmaroc King: when one player beats it, it ends in the partner's game too. To leave a boss out, list the ones you want under Advanced: **Boss kills** (or `--coop-boss-kill=...`).

**Less tested.** These three were checked live in parts only: thrown bombs, arrows and the boat cannon each way, but not a bomb into an Armos Knight's mouth, breakable walls under co-op, or light arrows; and Gohdan's relay with the fight forced, not a full fight from start to finish.
