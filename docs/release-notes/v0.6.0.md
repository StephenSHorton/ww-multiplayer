## Wind Waker Multiplayer v0.6.0

See your friend's Link in your game, live. Download the app, double-click it, and it walks you through the rest.

**New in v0.6.0: Keese and boss kills.** Keese join the shared enemies by default, next to the Moblins, Bokoblins and ChuChus. In co-op, a boss one player beats counts for the whole crew: Gohma, Kalle Demos, Molgera, Jalhalla and the Helmaroc King end in the partner's game too, so nobody has to fight them twice. Co-op also keeps more of each dungeon in step (switches, doors, statues and the story's own events in the Tower of the Gods, the Forbidden Woods, the Earth and Wind Temples, the Forsaken Fortress and Ganon's Tower), and each player sets their own boss-kill and refresh choices in the save-file panel.

**Also new:**
- **Up to four players** see each other as Links: your game shows the three others, each in their tunic color. A fifth player and more show as a simpler stand-in.
- **Their boats.** A player who sails shows in their King of Red Lions, with the sail in their tunic color and the cannon or salvage crane when they take it out. A boat they leave behind stays where they left it, on the sea and in the Tower of the Gods, where it floats on your game's water.
- **More of each player is drawn:** a name tag over each player, their sword and shield (the Mirror Shield too), the Master Sword's scabbard, the hair under casual clothes, and their spot on your sea chart.
- **A soft reset** (Start+B+X, or Dolphin's Reset) goes back to the title screen, as in the unmodded game, instead of restarting the whole game. Once you load your file the other players come back by themselves.
- **A player in another stage** (a house, a cave, a dungeon) is hidden in your game until they are back in yours, with any number of players. The host's server also sends each player's moves only to the players in the same stage, which saves bandwidth.
- **Compressed discs:** a `.rvz`, `.wia` or `.gcz` disc works too. The app converts it with the DolphinTool that comes with your Dolphin before patching.
- **A new look for the app:** a sidebar, a Settings page, and light and dark themes.
- **A dedicated server:** `ww-multiplayer server` runs a session with no player on its machine, for example over Tailscale or a port forward. The owner key it prints lets one player host its lobby (the Join form's "Advanced: dedicated server owner key"), and `ww-multiplayer status <address>` checks whether it is up. A dedicated server has no co-op. The repository's `docs/11-self-hosting.md` has the steps.

**You need** [Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org/) (a recent stable build) and **your own legitimate copy of The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker (NTSC-U, GZLE01)** as a `.iso`, `.ciso`, `.rvz`, `.wia` or `.gcz` file.

**Everyone must run the same version.** A host turns away a player on an incompatible version with a message saying to update (v0.6.0 can't play with v0.5.x or older), and a player whose built-in mods differ from the host's.

**Upgrading?** Your disc has to be patched again, because the game code this version adds has changed. The app does it on its first start after the update, from the original disc you chose in Setup (or it opens Setup so you can pick it). On the command line, run `patch` on your vanilla (unmodified) disc again. If you boot the patched disc from Dolphin's game list instead of from the app, use Dolphin 2609 or newer, or turn on Dolphin's memory override (MEM1 48 MB) yourself. Dolphin save states taken on an older version are refused: load your in-game save and take a new state.

### Windows

1. Download `ww-multiplayer.exe` and double-click it. A console window may flash for a moment; the app window then opens.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen; the exe isn't code-signed), click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. The first time, the app opens on **Setup**: your name and tunic color, your Wind Waker disc (the app writes a patched copy next to it and never changes your original) and Dolphin (found for you, or Browse…).
4. **Host**, or **Join**: pick the host's session from the list when you're on the same network, or paste the join code (`WW-XXXX-XXXX`) or address the host shares. Everyone meets in the lobby; when the others are Ready, the host presses **Start** and each player's Dolphin starts with the patched game.

The window needs Microsoft's WebView2 runtime, which Windows 11 and current Windows 10 have; without it the app offers to install it. If Windows asks, allow `ww-multiplayer.exe` through the firewall.

### macOS (Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 12 or later)

1. Download `ww-multiplayer-macos.zip` and open it (Safari usually unzips it for you). Move the `WW Multiplayer` folder somewhere permanent, such as Applications.
2. Double-click `WW Multiplayer.app`. If macOS says it can't verify the app (it isn't notarized by Apple): Control-click (right-click) the app, choose **Open**, then **Open** again. On macOS 15 or later, after the first warning go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**.
3. The first time, the app opens on **Setup**: your name and tunic color, your Wind Waker disc, and Dolphin. Press **Prepare Dolphin** once (and again after each Dolphin update): it makes a copy at `~/Applications/Dolphin.app` that the app is allowed to read, and leaves your original Dolphin alone. No password needed.
4. **Host** or **Join**, then **Start** from the lobby, as on Windows.

### Co-op (optional)

Shared progress is off unless the host turns it on: in the lobby the host picks **New world** or **Continue**, and the app guides everyone through binding a save file. Plain multiplayer needs none of it.

While you play, the Session page's **What co-op shares** card shows what the world shares, and the host can press **Edit** to turn story, items, switches, puzzles, kills, small keys, hearts, magic, live switches or merging on and off for everyone without restarting (the app remembers the choice for the next session). The **Room items** card shows what the shared world holds: items and equipment, songs and pearls, Triforce shards, charts, bag sizes and the heart containers and pieces taken. With two players, the partner's row in **Players** has **Warp to**.

Co-op also closes a few ways to be paid twice: a boss's Heart Container counts once for each player, side-quest progress that pays out (Orca's sword training, Koboli's mail sorting, the pigs in Outset's pen) is shared, and a sunken treasure one player pulls up can no longer be pulled up again by the partner.

**Reporting a bug.** The **Bug report** button in Settings' Help card saves one zip with your newest session logs, Dolphin's own log (when Dolphin writes one) and the app's diagnostics, and **Show folder** opens where it is. Your home folder, IP addresses, join codes, host names you joined on (`•••.duckdns.org:25570`), tokens and a dedicated server's owner key are masked, and your disc, saves and co-op worlds are never in it. Nothing is uploaded: attach the zip to your issue. From a terminal, `ww-multiplayer bug-report` writes the same zip.

IP addresses are hidden on screen by default (`•••.•••.•••.42`), so you can stream the app; the host's share card has Show and Copy.

### Advanced: terminal menu and command line

The same program has a terminal menu and a command line. Windows: `.\ww-multiplayer.exe tui`, `.\ww-multiplayer.exe patch <iso>`, `.\ww-multiplayer.exe host [name]`, `.\ww-multiplayer.exe join <host-ip|join-code> [name]`. macOS: in Terminal, `cd` into the unzipped `WW Multiplayer` folder, run `xattr -cr .` once to clear the download quarantine, then `./ww-multiplayer tui` (or `patch`, `host`, `join`); `./scripts/setup-mac-dolphin.sh` does what Prepare Dolphin does. `ww-multiplayer version` prints the version. `ww-multiplayer status <address>` exits 0 when a server is up, 1 when nothing answers or it is not a ww-multiplayer server, and 3 for an older version. A few switches turn the new pieces off for a session: `WW_BOATS=off`, `WW_NAMETAGS=off`, `WW_EQUIP=off`, `WW_SEACHART=off`, `WW_HAIR=off`. Not on by default yet: `WW_PROJECTILES=on`, set on every player, shows the other players' bombs, arrows and cannonballs in your game.

### Verify your download

`SHA256SUMS` lists the SHA-256 of every file above. macOS / Linux: `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing`. Windows (PowerShell): `Get-FileHash ww-multiplayer.exe` and compare with the matching line.
