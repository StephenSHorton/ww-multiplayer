## Wind Waker Multiplayer v0.7.0

**Widescreen.**

- **Objects no longer pop in at the screen edges** when you play with Dolphin's widescreen hack. The game used to hide people, grass, trees and shadows in the outer strips of a 16:9 picture before they reached the edge; now it draws everything you can see.
- **Native widescreen, on by default at 16:9.** The game itself draws the wider picture, with no Dolphin hack: the app launches Dolphin with its hack off and Force 16:9. The HUD sits at the screen edges and keeps its round shape, menus, the map, the minigames' displays, the title movie and the ending movie are drawn 4:3 in the centre or widened, and the lens flare and the stars keep their place. Pick another aspect (16:10, 21:9) or Off on the Display card; Off gives the old 4:3 picture, and Dolphin's own hack still works with it.

**Upgrading:** the game code changed, so **patch your disc again** (the app does it by itself on its first start after the update; on the command line, run `patch` on your vanilla disc again). Save states from older versions are refused.
