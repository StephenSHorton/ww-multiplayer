## Wind Waker Multiplayer v0.7.0

See your friend's Link in your game, live. Download the app, double-click it, and it walks you through the rest.

**Everyone must update to v0.7.0.** The network protocol changed, so a v0.7.0 host turns away v0.6.x and older players with a message saying to update. The app re-patches your disc by itself, and save states from older versions are refused. Details are under "Upgrading" below.

### New in v0.7.0: native widescreen

The game itself now draws a wide picture. It does not rely on Dolphin's widescreen hack, which only widened the 3D and stretched everything else.

- **16:9, 16:10, 21:9 or a custom aspect** (whole numbers, such as 64:27 for a 2560x1080 screen). Pick it in **Settings > Display > Widescreen**. It takes effect at the next **Start**.
- **On by default at 16:9.** To play in 4:3 as before, pick **Off** in the same place.
- **The HUD and menus keep their true proportions.** Hearts and buttons are round and text isn't stretched. The HUD sits at the edges of the screen: hearts, magic and the minimap on the left, buttons and rupees on the right. Dims, fades and menu backdrops cover the full width.
- **Objects no longer pop in at the screen edges.** The game used to hide people, grass, trees and shadows in the outer strips of a wide picture before they reached the edge. Now it draws everything you can see, with Dolphin's widescreen hack too.
- **Movies are pillarboxed**: the title movie and the staff roll play at 4:3 in the centre, not stretched.
- **The sun's lens flare, the stars around the moon and the telescope's moon and star checks** follow the wide picture.
- **You don't need Dolphin's widescreen hack.** The app turns the hack off for the Dolphin it launches and sets Dolphin's aspect to match, only for that session: your Dolphin settings are not changed. Set the aspect in the app, not in Dolphin's graphics window. If you change Dolphin's aspect while playing, the picture is wrong until the next Start.
- **From the command line or the terminal menu**, the game stays 4:3 as before. With Dolphin's widescreen hack on, the pop-in fix still applies, and the name tags now stay over the other players' heads.

The Picto Box viewfinder frame stays stretched in widescreen, but the photos come out right.

### New in v0.7.0: co-op that just works

Update, press Host or Join, press Start, load a save. That should be all.

- **Your disc stays current.** After an update the app re-patches your disc by itself from the original you chose in Setup, while you wait on the Play page. It does this for joiners too, in the lobby. Pressing Start during a re-patch waits for it, then launches. If you moved your original disc, the app looks for it next to the patched one and in Dolphin's game folders before asking. If your own Dolphin has the disc open, the app asks you to close it.
- **Co-op is on by default.** The host's **Co-op world** choice in the lobby is **Automatic**: the world follows the save the host loads. A save from a world on this computer continues it. A save not yet in any world starts a new one from it. A save tied to a world from another computer starts that world here. A second-quest save plays without co-op. **Off** plays together without a shared world, and the app remembers it. **New world** and **Continue** are still there for picking a specific world.
- **Joiners follow the host.** A joiner's save joins the host's world with no questions. A save tied to some other world is cleared of that tie first, and a save with progress the world lacks merges it in. The joiner's own worlds on their computer stay untouched.
- **No in-game save needed.** When a save joins a world, the app writes that into your memory card after Dolphin closes. It backs up the card first and checks the result. Before, the tie was lost unless you saved in game. This works with Dolphin's default memory card (a GCI folder); with a raw memory card file, save in game as before.
- **Never stuck on Starting.** The game starts at once, and the world joins in when it's ready. If co-op can't run for someone (a second-quest save, for example), every player's window says why in one line, and you still see each other.
- **A first-time player** gets an empty save file, so the game doesn't stop to ask to create one.
- **A different app version** in the lobby shows a line naming who is on which version.

### Also new

- **The Session page's sea chart is the real Great Sea**, with every player at their true spot. A player in a house, cave or dungeon is pinned to that island, with the place's name.
- **Each player's row** shows their hearts, magic and rupees and where they are (a dungeon and room, a sea square, or Loading), with or without co-op.
- **An Activity tab** beside Chat lists who got what, where ("Medli got the Grappling Hook in Dragon Roost Cavern"). At the end of a co-op session, a recap you can copy.
- **A Dungeons section** in the co-op **Room items** card: each dungeon's map, compass, big key, boss and heart container.
- **The other players on the title screen.** Each connected player's Link stands next to PRESS START with their name tag, and the file select screen lists who is here. `WW_LOBBY=off` hides both.
- **Smoother remote Links on a bad connection.** A short delay buffer and a little prediction keep a remote Link moving through a lag spike instead of stopping and jumping. The moves also take less bandwidth.
- **Armos and Darknuts** are shared enemies by default, like the Moblins, Bokoblins, ChuChus and Keese.
- **Co-op for three or more players**: each partner is tracked on their own, and **Warp to** names the partner to warp to.
- **Co-op shares more:**
  - When two players stand at the same chest, only one of them gets it.
  - A plain enemy one player kills shrinks away in the partner's game while both are in the room.
  - Chests, boulders, bushes, barricades, pickups and flower houses your partner already opened or cleared update in your game without a room change.
- **Co-op leaves your clothes and your ending alone.** The hero's clothes are your own: a partner in them no longer puts you in them. From the credits on, the player who finished leaves the co-op world, and the others play on.

**Also fixed:**
- Name tags stay over the other players' heads with Dolphin's widescreen hack on (they used to drift sideways and stretch).
- The other players' tunic sleeves stay in their tunic's color instead of turning another hue.
- Fewer hitches in some rooms: the stand-in actors for extra players no longer keep respawning.
- Session log files now mask IP addresses and join codes, like the screen does.
- macOS: Setup explains what **Prepare Dolphin** does before you press it, and **Remove the copy** undoes it. The app no longer attaches to another Dolphin that is already running.

**Less tested.** Widescreen was checked live on one Windows PC: the world and the pop-in fix, the HUD at the edges (at 16:9, 16:10 and 21:9), the minimap, the pause menus, the file select, the Picto Box, the title movie and the staff roll, the lens flare and the stars, Sploosh Kaboom and the cannon game's display, and two-player sessions at 16:9 and with widescreen off. Not yet checked live: custom aspects, the telescope's moon and star checks, the boat race, Orca's game, the auction, and macOS. Changing the aspect (or turning it off) mid-game reaches the HUD and the movies at the next area you load. The co-op changes were checked live on one PC with two players: an update from an older version, the automatic re-patch, Host + Join + Start, a host save from a missing world, and a fresh joiner. They have not been checked across two machines, and the first-time empty save has not been run live. If something misbehaves, **Widescreen: Off** and the lobby's **Co-op world: Off** turn each one off, and the **Bug report** button in Settings makes a zip to attach to an issue.

**You need** [Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org/) (a recent stable build) and **your own legitimate copy of The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker (NTSC-U, GZLE01)** as a `.iso`, `.ciso`, `.rvz`, `.wia` or `.gcz` file.

**Everyone must run v0.7.0.** It does not play with v0.6.1 or older: a host turns away an older version with a message saying to update, and turns away a player whose built-in mods differ from the host's.

**Upgrading from v0.6.x or older?** The game code changed, so your disc needs patching again. The app does it by itself from the original you chose in Setup. On the command line, run `patch` on your vanilla disc again. Save states from older versions are refused: load your save from the memory card instead.

### Windows

1. Download `ww-multiplayer.exe` and double-click it. A console window may flash for a moment; the app window then opens.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen; the exe isn't code-signed), click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. The first time, the app opens on **Setup**: your name and tunic color, your Wind Waker disc (the app writes a patched copy next to it and never changes your original) and Dolphin (found for you, or Browse…).
4. **Host**, or **Join**: pick the host's session from the list when you're on the same network, or paste the join code (`WW-XXXX-XXXX`) or address the host shares. Everyone meets in the lobby; when the others are Ready, the host presses **Start** and each player's Dolphin starts with the patched game.

The window needs Microsoft's WebView2 runtime, which Windows 11 and current Windows 10 have; without it the app offers to install it. If Windows asks, allow `ww-multiplayer.exe` through the firewall.

### macOS (Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 12 or later)

1. Download `ww-multiplayer-macos.zip` and open it (Safari usually unzips it for you). Move the `WW Multiplayer` folder somewhere permanent, such as Applications.
2. Double-click `WW Multiplayer.app`. If macOS says it can't verify the app (it isn't notarized by Apple): Control-click (right-click) the app, choose **Open**, then **Open** again. On macOS 15 or later, after the first warning go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**.
3. The first time, the app opens on **Setup**: your name and tunic color, your Wind Waker disc, and Dolphin. Press **Prepare Dolphin** once (and again after each Dolphin update): it makes a copy at `~/Applications/Dolphin.app` that the app is allowed to read, and leaves your original Dolphin alone. No password needed. **Remove the copy** undoes it.
4. **Host** or **Join**, then **Start** from the lobby, as on Windows.

### Playing over the internet

On the same home network, joining needs nothing more. Across the internet, the easy way is a free VPN with no router setup: put everyone on [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com) or [Toru](https://stephenshorton.github.io/toru/) (Windows), and join the host's address on it. Forwarding TCP port 25565 on the host's router works too. The repository's `docs/online.md` has the details, and `docs/11-self-hosting.md` covers a dedicated server.

### Co-op

Co-op is on by default: with **Co-op world: Automatic**, the world follows the save the host loads, and joiners join it (see above). The host can pick **Off** in the lobby for plain multiplayer.

While you play, the Session page's **What co-op shares** card shows what the world shares, and the host can press **Edit** to turn story, items, switches, puzzles, kills, small keys, hearts, magic, live switches or merging on and off for everyone without restarting (the app remembers the choice for the next session). The **Room items** card shows what the shared world holds: items and equipment, songs and pearls, Triforce shards, charts, bag sizes, the heart containers and pieces taken, and now each dungeon's items. The partner's row in **Players** has **Warp to**.

Co-op also closes a few ways to be paid twice: a boss's Heart Container counts once for each player, side-quest progress that pays out (Orca's sword training, Koboli's mail sorting, the pigs in Outset's pen) is shared, a sunken treasure one player pulls up can no longer be pulled up again by the partner, and a chest goes to one player only.

**Reporting a bug.** The **Bug report** button in Settings' Help card saves one zip with your newest session logs, Dolphin's own log (when Dolphin writes one) and the app's diagnostics, and **Show folder** opens where it is. Your home folder, IP addresses, join codes, host names you joined on (`•••.duckdns.org:25570`), tokens and a dedicated server's owner key are masked, and your disc, saves and co-op worlds are never in it. Nothing is uploaded: attach the zip to your issue. From a terminal, `ww-multiplayer bug-report` writes the same zip.

IP addresses are hidden on screen and in the session logs by default (`•••.•••.•••.42`), so you can stream the app; the host's share card has Show and Copy.

### Advanced: terminal menu and command line

The same program has a terminal menu and a command line. Windows: `.\ww-multiplayer.exe tui`, `.\ww-multiplayer.exe patch <iso>`, `.\ww-multiplayer.exe host [name]`, `.\ww-multiplayer.exe join <host-ip|join-code> [name]`. macOS: in Terminal, `cd` into the unzipped `WW Multiplayer` folder, run `xattr -cr .` once to clear the download quarantine, then `./ww-multiplayer tui` (or `patch`, `host`, `join`); `./scripts/setup-mac-dolphin.sh` does what Prepare Dolphin does. `ww-multiplayer version` prints the version. `ww-multiplayer status <address>` exits 0 when a server is up, 1 when nothing answers or it is not a ww-multiplayer server, and 3 for an older version. The command line and terminal menu don't re-patch your disc or pick a co-op world by themselves: run `patch` after an update and pass `--coop` as before. Native widescreen is set in the app only.

A few switches turn pieces off for a session: `WW_BOATS=off`, `WW_NAMETAGS=off`, `WW_EQUIP=off`, `WW_SEACHART=off`, `WW_HAIR=off`, `WW_LOBBY=off`, `WW_PROJECTILES=off`. On `host`, `--coop-live-switches=off` keeps co-op's switches at room changes and `--coop-placed-kill=off` stops sharing plain enemy kills. `WW_UDP=1` on every player (and the host) sends moves over UDP instead of TCP, which can help on a lossy connection; it is new and off by default.

### Verify your download

`SHA256SUMS` lists the SHA-256 of every file above. macOS / Linux: `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing`. Windows (PowerShell): `Get-FileHash ww-multiplayer.exe` and compare with the matching line.
